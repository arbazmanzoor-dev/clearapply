Accessible job applications

Job applications that talk you through every step.

ClearApply is built for people who use a screen reader. Every button, field and error message tells you what it is and what to do next, so you can apply on your own.

How ClearApply helps

label

Every button has a name

You hear “Upload your resume, button”, never just “button”.

error

Errors tell you the fix

If something is wrong, you hear exactly what to change, like “Please enter an email address, like name@example.com”.

record_voice_over

A voice guide on every page

Each page explains itself and tells you what to do next. Turn it off if you prefer your own screen reader.

mic

Speak your answers

Every field has a Speak button. Say your answer and it is typed in and read back to you. Before you submit, you can hear all your answers in one go.
