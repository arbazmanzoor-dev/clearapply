About this project

Why we built ClearApply

ClearApply is a Design Thinking prototype. It redesigns an online job application for blind people who use a screen reader.

The problem

A blind woman who depends on a screen reader for daily independent tasks, including job applications, banking and shopping, cannot complete an online job application because its buttons and icons carry no text labels or alt text, leaving her screen reader unable to tell her what each element does. This happens because the design team assumed a visually clear, well-organised interface is automatically accessible, without checking what information actually reaches a non-visual user.

How to try it

  • Start on the home page, choose the Junior Web Developer job and apply.
  • Use only the keyboard: Tab to move, Enter to press.
  • The voice guide speaks each page and each item. Alt+R repeats the instructions, Alt+V turns it on or off, Esc stops it.
  • To hear it with a real screen reader, turn the voice guide off and use VoiceOver (Mac, iPhone), TalkBack (Android) or NVDA (Windows, free).
