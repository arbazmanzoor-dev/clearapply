ClearApply Design Thinking · Experiment 2 · Prototype
Accessible job application prototype

Every button tells her what it does.

ClearApply is a redesigned online job application for blind people who use a screen reader. On a typical form, the screen reader just says “button” or “image”. In ClearApply, every button, image and error message is labelled, so she can apply for a job on her own.

Problem statement

A blind woman who depends on a screen reader for daily independent tasks, including job applications, banking and shopping, cannot complete an online job application because its buttons and icons carry no text labels or alt text, leaving her screen reader unable to tell her what each element does. This happens because the design team assumed a visually clear, well-organised interface is automatically accessible, without checking what information actually reaches a non-visual user.

How to read and test it

  • The grey panel on the right of each screen, “What the screen reader says”, shows what a blind user would hear on that screen.
  • Try it with only the keyboard: press Tab to move and Enter to press.
  • To hear it for real, turn on a screen reader: VoiceOver on Mac or iPhone, TalkBack on Android, or NVDA (free) on Windows.
